# Ethos Voice

Applies to every email, recap, document, and message. Run `scripts/lint_voice.py` on drafts before handoff.

## Principles
- Direct without being curt. Warm without being saccharine.
- Specific over vague: real dates, names, numbers. "Tuesday" beats "soon."
- Active voice. "We completed the integration," not "the integration was completed."
- Open with the point. End with a clear next step or ask.
- Confident, not apologetic. If something is late, say so with a revised date.
- Should not read as AI-written. Tight content, natural phrasing.

## Matt's outbound tone
Leader voice: brevity, clarity, and directness matter more than warmth. Direct but fair. Match warmth to the relationship (a long-time client on an overdue item gets softer framing than a first-touch sales email).

## Hard rules
- No emojis.
- No em dashes. Use commas, colons, periods, or separate sentences.
- Hyphens and semicolons sparingly.
- Emails: 2 to 5 sentences for most replies. Three paragraphs is usually too many.
- Every email draft ends with the hard-coded signature block defined in `standards/tools.md` (Microsoft 365). Do not write it any other way.

## Banned phrases (and variants)
- I hope this helps / Hope this helps
- Let me know if you have questions / Please don't hesitate to (reach out, contact)
- It's worth noting
- As discussed / As mentioned
- Thank you for reaching out
- Hope you're doing well / Hope this finds you well / I trust this email finds you well
- I just wanted to... (drop "just")
- Sorry to bother you
- Thanks in advance
- Stacked hedges ("I think maybe we could potentially")

## Banned words in SDDs and design docs
robust, seamless, leverage, utilize, ensure, streamline, comprehensive, as needed, where appropriate

## Examples
Bad: "Hi John, I hope this email finds you well! I just wanted to follow up on our discussion from last week regarding the timeline..."
Good: "John, checking in on the timeline. Is end of month still workable for your team?"

Bad: "Sorry for the delay! We've been super swamped but we're working hard to get this to you ASAP."
Good: "We're behind on the design doc. Targeting Thursday for delivery, with a draft to you by EOD Wednesday."
